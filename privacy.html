Privacy Policy

Avatar Income · Last updated 26 July 2026

This covers what happens to your information when you message Avatar Income on Facebook or Instagram. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Who runs this

Avatar Income is a one-person business operated by Carl Griffin in San Antonio, Texas. There is no team and no third party you haven't been told about below.

What is collected

When you send a message to the Avatarincome Facebook Page or the @avatarincome Instagram account:

  • The text of your message
  • An ID that Meta assigns to you for this Page. It is specific to this Page and cannot be used to identify you anywhere else.
  • The date and time of the message
  • The replies sent back to you

What is not collected

This is the shorter and more useful list. Not collected, because the app is not permitted to request it:

  • Your name, profile photo, or profile information
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your friends, followers, or anything else on your account
  • Anything you post publicly, outside of a comment you leave on our posts
  • Payment details of any kind — those never touch this system

Replies are generated by an AI

Most replies are written by an AI assistant, not by a person typing in real time. If you ask whether you are talking to a bot, it will tell you plainly. Some conversations are handed to Carl to answer personally — see below.

To generate a reply, your message and the recent conversation are sent to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, that content is not used to train their models.

When a person reads your message

Some conversations are flagged for Carl to answer personally rather than being handled automatically. That happens when a message involves:

  • Payments, refunds, or account problems
  • Anything suggesting a crisis, someone's safety, or a child's welfare
  • Anything the assistant is not confident it should answer

In those cases the AI stops replying and Carl reads the conversation. This is deliberate: a confident wrong answer from software is worse than a slower answer from a human.

Where it is stored

Messages are stored in a database hosted by Cloudflare on servers in the United States. Access is limited to Carl. Nothing is sold, rented, shared for advertising, or handed to data brokers — not now and not later.

How long it is kept

Conversations are kept so the assistant can remember context between messages, and so Carl can follow up on anything handed to him. They are kept until you ask for them to be deleted, or until they are no longer needed.

Deleting your data

Email carlgriffin57@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my data, from any address, and say which Facebook or Instagram account you messaged from.

Your conversation history and contact record are deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply confirming it is done. You do not have to give a reason, and nothing is kept afterward.

You can also remove this app's access from your Facebook settings under Settings → Apps and Websites. That stops future messages being processed, but does not delete what already exists — email for that.

Third parties involved

  • Meta — delivers your message; governed by Meta's own privacy policy
  • Cloudflare — stores the conversation
  • Anthropic — generates the reply text

That is the complete list.

If something goes wrong

If your data is exposed in a way it shouldn't be, you will be told directly — not buried in a status page update.

If you are in crisis

This is not a crisis service and no one monitors messages around the clock. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, please call or text 988 in the US (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911. Outside the US, contact your local emergency number.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who it is shared with, the date at the top changes and the change is described here.
